# MVE Document — artifact contract

MVE means Minimum Viable Evidence. Produce factual fragments, inventories, mappings, and questions for independent authors; never submission-ready prose, CV bullets, polished headlines, or letter wording. Follow [document-requirements.md](document-requirements.md) for current-rule checks and document constraints.

Adapt the following to the requested scope. Mark unknowns and unassessed documents. For **Build first**, include only the summary, evidence-building actions, and consistency notes; do not imply the application is ready.

- **Application summary:** pathway, readiness, mandatory and selected optional criteria, evidence counts, recommenders, strongest proof, next best upgrade, and dated official-source checks or retrieval limits.
- **Form-response facts:** relevant career journey, attributable achievements and innovation, recognition, genuine UK plans and contribution, strongest supported metrics, and an ordered factual narrative. Match the live form's prompts; do not invent specificity.
- **CV inventory:** exact role/title/company/dates; action, scope, outcome, metric, and proof for relevant achievements. Include skills, products, open source, publications, speaking, and awards only where relevant. The applicant chooses wording and layout.
- **Recommender selection and briefings:** selected/backup/cut candidates with reasons based on expertise and knowledge gates. For each selected author, record credentials, knowledge duration/context, claims personally substantiated, sources of that knowledge, distinct examples, and missing contact or credential material. Supply only facts that author can independently confirm.
- **Evidence plan:** for each document, a working identifier, single assigned criterion, Core/Backup/Build/Cut role, claim facts, timeframe, personal contribution, proof sources, corroborator if any, and why the proof establishes significance. Map documents to criteria and verify readiness using the requirements; no double-counting or cosmetic duplication.
- **Evidence-building actions:** unresolved gap, genuine action, expected proof, and realistic dependency or timing. Build/Cut items do not count toward readiness.
- **Cross-document consistency:** dates, names, metrics, attribution, author knowledge, and public-profile discrepancies, with the affected documents and unresolved questions. Do not assume a public profile is correct.

End with the next useful action. The applicant and recommenders independently write their respective documents before review. The artifact can be saved and reused without requiring a new conversation. No submission or messaging follows from producing it.
