# Targeted evidence extraction

Use these prompts only for material unknowns in the requested planning scope. Reuse the profile or equivalent context; do not run a fixed interview or ask every question. Outputs remain factual fragments. Apply [document-requirements.md](document-requirements.md) for requirements and the root readiness gate.

## Evidence and attribution

Establish what happened, when, what the applicant personally did, the outcome, and the source that proves it. For vague leadership claims, ask what decisions or work they owned. For growth, ask the starting value, ending value, period, and source. Identify who can corroborate the contribution.

Ask why the result matters for the assigned criterion: significant contributions can be inside the product-led company; work beyond occupation must advance the field beyond the applicant's occupation. Rate evidence Strong for direct fit, attribution, concrete outcomes, and corroboration; Moderate for relevant but incomplete proof; Weak for vague, mismatched, or unsupported claims. Metrics help where meaningful but are not the only proof.

Prefer the strongest single criterion assignment. Distinct documents about one achievement need genuinely different proof; reframing is insufficient. If a gap remains, identify actual evidence-building work, expected corroboration, and realistic timing instead of forcing readiness.

## Recommenders

Compare plausible candidates against the official expertise and knowledge-duration gates, then direct knowledge, specificity, and useful perspective. Do not reject internal authors or older work automatically. Ask what each person directly observed, how they know it, and which distinct examples they can truthfully substantiate. A title alone does not establish a strong recommender.

Collect missing credentials and required contact details for selected authors. Do not require letters to cite numbered documents or collectively cover every criterion unless current guidance does. Each author independently writes their own letter.

## Document-specific gaps

- **Form response:** relevant career journey, most consequential achievements, recognition, and genuine UK plans addressing the live form. Ask what makes the trajectory distinctive; do not require ornamental organisation names or activities.
- **CV:** exact role/title/company/dates and relevant achievement facts with proof; relevant skills, products, open source, publications, talks, and awards. Compare discrepancies with other materials without assuming a public profile is correct.
- **Evidence document:** claim facts, applicant contribution, timeframe, outcome, proof inventory, provenance, and criterion connection. Ask for missing screenshots, analytics, confirmations, or public records only when useful.

For overlooked proof, suggest targeted sources such as conference listings, project analytics, publication citations, press coverage, or company records. These are discovery options, not eligibility requirements or a mandatory search checklist.
