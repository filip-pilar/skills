# GTV Review Report — artifact contract

Report only on the supplied scope. Use [review-criteria.md](review-criteria.md) for requirements and evidence standards. Adapt the layout; do not repeat empty checklists for each document or manufacture criticism.

Include:

- **Scope:** review date, pathway and criteria interpretation, documents reviewed, relevant planning artifact, dated official-source checks, and unavailable information.
- **Overall assessment:** supported strengths, material weaknesses, and the most useful next improvement. Give Submit after edits, Revise before submission, or Build evidence first only within the reviewed scope. A partial review cannot establish readiness of unseen documents; say when no material edits were identified.
- **Per-document findings:** Strong/Adequate/Needs Work/Weak rating with reasons, supporting passages, material requirement or evidence issues, and actionable revision goals. Apply the relevant CV, form, letter, and evidence checks without printing every successful check. For evidence, assess assigned-criterion fit, attribution, provenance, and significance; for letters, expertise, knowledge, specificity, required elements, and distinct examples.
- **Consistency:** consequential differences in dates, names, titles, metrics, attribution, author knowledge, and public profiles, identifying the affected sources. State the limits when cross-document comparison is unavailable.
- **Priority fixes:** issue, evidence, consequence, and revision goal in severity order. Separate changes achievable with existing material from missing evidence that requires genuine work. Supported skeptical questions and distinctive case facts belong beside the relevant finding rather than in duplicate reviews.
- **Iteration:** if earlier versions are available, identify verified improvements and remaining issues; otherwise distinguish reported progress from observed changes. Compare with an MVE only if supplied.

Describe generic, duplicated, inconsistent, or unsupported wording only when it affects the case. Style cannot establish authorship or AI use. Give questions and revision goals, never replacement sentences. Include post-submission process or timing only when relevant and currently verified. End with the useful next step and material evidence limits.
