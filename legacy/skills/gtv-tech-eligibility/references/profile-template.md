# GTV Profile — artifact contract

Produce a reusable assessment in factual fragments, not application prose. Adapt layout to the case; omit inapplicable fields, identify unknowns, and do not delay a useful assessment to fill optional sections. Apply [criteria.md](criteria.md) rather than duplicating or inventing requirements.

Include:

- **Pathway and readiness:** Talent or Promise with reasons and relevant tradeoffs; Apply now, Build first, or No current GTV route. Mark provisional conclusions. Capture case-thesis facts, strongest proof, next best upgrade, and next action.
- **Relevant background:** role, digital-technology experience, sector, achievements, and location or immigration status where it affects advice.
- **Criteria map:** mandatory recognition and two selected optional criteria, with supporting facts, evidence strength, and material gaps. Include a backup criterion only if useful.
- **Evidence inventory:** item, assigned criterion, Core/Backup/Build/Cut role, strength, metric or proof source, personal attribution, and missing corroboration. Retain considered items so exclusions are understandable.
- **Recommenders:** name and role, established-expert basis, duration and context of knowledge, specific claims they can substantiate, and selection rationale. Identify the recommended three and useful backups where supported.
- **Gaps and actions:** material risks, genuine evidence-building work, expected proof, and timing derived from that work. Do not imply an activity or platform guarantees acceptance.
- **Consistency:** exact role dates, names, metrics, and material public-profile discrepancies that later documents must resolve.
- **Sources and next step:** dated official checks and unavailable sources; relevant live-verified process or timing, otherwise identify what was not checked. Explain how the profile supports document planning and can be saved or reused in the current or another conversation.

Keep the planning-only status clear without repeating disclaimers in every section.
