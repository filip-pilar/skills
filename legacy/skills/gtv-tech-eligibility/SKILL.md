# GTV Eligibility Assessment

Assess the user's evidence and pathway, then produce a reusable GTV Profile.
Provide guidance, not a guarantee or legal advice; make that distinction clear
once without repeating disclaimers throughout the conversation.

## Boundaries and current requirements

Provide assessment, questions, factual inventories, and planning fragments only.
Never draft or rewrite application prose: the applicant authors their CV, form
responses, and evidence text; each recommender authors their own letter. Verify
Tech Nation's current authorship guidance before advising on submission. If it
cannot be retrieved, disclose that limit without claiming a current rule was checked.

Before stating current criteria, process, document limits, fees, or timings, check
GOV.UK and Appendix Global Talent using the official links in
[criteria.md](references/criteria.md). The Immigration Rules own the legal taxonomy.
Explain material differences from simplified guidance. Reuse sources already
checked in this conversation when still applicable; do not invent cached values.

## Assess what is known

Use the conversation and supplied CV, profile, or other evidence before asking
questions. Establish relevant work, career stage, technical or business expertise,
recognition, attributable impact, corroboration, and immigration context where it
affects the advice. Ask only about gaps that could change the assessment, grouping
related questions when helpful. Do not require an opening interview, confirmation
of unambiguous extracted facts, or a particular input artifact.

Read [sector-guide.md](references/sector-guide.md) when sector or role fit needs
assessment, especially consulting, outsourcing, hybrid, or hardware-heavy work.
Do not accept or reject someone on job title or employer type alone. Consider
plausible product work, side projects, open source, community work, and research
before concluding no current route. Use supplied evidence to cover these avenues;
do not force an exhaustive interview when the relevant facts are already clear.

Read [criteria.md](references/criteria.md) for pathway and evidence mapping.
Distinguish recognition as a leader from potential leadership at an early career
stage; years of experience are an indicator, not a mechanical cutoff. Keep Promise
and Talent's evidential differences separate from work permissions. State the
live-verified settlement difference when recommending a pathway, or say it was not
verified. For a user already in the UK, check relevant switching/status implications;
never imply endorsement or review alone extends immigration permission.

## Map the case

Map mandatory recognition and the strongest two of the four optional criteria:
innovation, work beyond occupation, significant contributions, and academic
contributions. The founder/executive and employee innovation routes are alternatives
within one criterion. Apply the exact Talent or Promise wording from current rules.

Assess evidence by fit, personal attribution, specificity, corroboration, and
significance. Record strengths and gaps without turning illustrative sources,
platforms, metrics, or internal/external provenance into invented eligibility rules.
Identify plausible recommendation authors by established digital-technology expertise
and the required knowledge of the applicant's work; compare direct knowledge and
specificity rather than prestige or relationship labels alone.

Surface weak criteria promptly. Suggest genuine evidence-building work and targeted
sources to check only where they could strengthen the case. Derive timing from the
actual gap; no activity checklist, platform, or waiting period guarantees acceptance.
If no plausible GTV route remains, say so and point to current GOV.UK alternatives.

## Deliver the profile

Use [profile-template.md](references/profile-template.md) when producing the profile.
Preserve case-thesis facts as fragments, pathway, criteria mapping, evidence inventory,
recommenders, material gaps, consistent dates/names/metrics, strongest proof, next best
upgrade, and next action. Update an existing profile when useful; do not repeatedly
emit the entire artifact during discovery or withhold a coherent assessment to fill
optional sections.

Give an evidence-based readiness call:

- **Apply now:** mandatory recognition and two optional criteria have credible
  evidence and recommenders, with no obvious blocker.
- **Build first:** a plausible route has material evidence or recommender gaps.
- **No current GTV route:** the relevant avenues provide no credible evidence base yet.

If essential facts remain unknown, label the assessment provisional and ask the
specific missing question instead of forcing certainty. Deliver the completed
profile for reuse in document planning, with sources checked and material limits.
