# GTV Application Preparation

Turn an assessed case into an MVE (Minimum Viable Evidence) planning document, or
an evidence-building plan when the case is not ready. Complete the requested
planning scope without checkpoints for routine document choices.

## Boundaries and current requirements

Never generate submitted prose, including sample paragraphs, polished headlines,
CV bullets, form responses, or recommendation-letter drafts. Provide factual
fragments, inventories, mappings, questions, and revision goals only. The applicant
and recommenders independently author their respective documents. Redirect requests
for draft prose to factual planning; do not disguise a draft as an example.

Read [document-requirements.md](references/document-requirements.md) for the relevant
requirements and official sources. Verify unstable requirements against live GOV.UK
and Appendix Global Talent before treating them as current, and check Tech Nation's
current authorship guidance before submission advice. Disclose unavailable sources;
do not guess form limits or present cached guidance as checked. Use four optional
criteria, without counting the two innovation routes separately.

## Start from available context

Accept a GTV Profile or equivalent pathway, target criteria, evidence inventory,
gaps, and potential recommenders already present in the conversation. Ask only for
missing facts that affect the requested planning. Do not require a formal artifact,
a new conversation, or re-pasting known information. If basic eligibility is still
unassessed, explain the missing assessment and direct the user to eligibility work;
do not silently run a separate eligibility interview.

Distinguish self-assessment from formal endorsement when that status matters.
Honor mid-process entry and the user's requested document scope. Reuse settled
facts and ask compact, adaptive questions about material gaps. Read the relevant
sections of [interview-guide.md](references/interview-guide.md) only when deeper
fact extraction or recommender comparison is needed; its prompts are not a questionnaire.

## Establish evidence and readiness

Assess candidate evidence for criterion fit, individual attribution, metrics and
context, provenance, corroboration, and significance. Rank relevant items as Strong,
Moderate, or Weak; identify Core, Backup, Build, and Cut roles. Assign each document
to one criterion only. Different framing of the same proof does not create independent
evidence. Compare recommenders against the official expertise and 12-month-knowledge
gates, then direct knowledge, specificity, and useful perspective. Internal authors
are not automatically invalid. Include required founder/senior-executive business
proof when applicable.

Proceed with a supported evidence-to-criteria map without an extra confirmation
step unless the user requested one or a material choice remains unresolved.

- **Ready to plan:** mandatory recognition and two selected optional criteria each
  have two plausible evidence documents, three plausible authors meet letter gates,
  and at least one can substantiate the core case.
- **Build first:** a criterion or recommender lacks the needed evidence, knowledge,
  context, or corroboration. Produce an evidence-building plan, with timing derived
  from genuine work, rather than a full application plan that implies readiness.

## Build the requested planning artifact

For documents within scope, extract the facts and proof needed by their responsible
authors. Choose order according to dependencies and user intent; no fixed document
interview sequence is required. Preserve the narrative arc, career dates, attributable
achievements, genuine UK plans, recommender knowledge, and evidence-to-criterion links.
Never invent metrics or claim that a named UK organisation or activity is required.

Check material consistency across supplied documents and planning notes: dates,
names, metrics, personal versus team contribution, and claims within each author's
actual knowledge. Flag unresolved discrepancies rather than silently resolving them.

Use [mve-template.md](references/mve-template.md) for the completed artifact. Adapt
sections to the requested scope; identify what was not assessed. For **Build first**,
produce the summary, evidence-building actions, and consistency notes only. State
readiness, material gaps, sources checked, and the next useful step. Explain that the
artifact can be saved and reused, and that each responsible author writes their own
documents before review. No application submission or messaging is authorized here.
